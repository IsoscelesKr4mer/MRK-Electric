Common Electrical Emergencies We Handle

Electrical problems range from inconvenient to dangerous. Here are the most common emergencies we respond to:

Tripping Circuit Breakers

Repeatedly tripping breakers indicate an overloaded circuit, short circuit, or ground fault. This is a safety hazard that requires professional diagnosis.

Dead or Non-Working Outlets

Outlets that suddenly stop working may indicate a tripped GFCI, broken outlet, or a wiring problem that needs immediate attention.

Burning Smell or Visible Sparks

A burning smell or sparks from outlets, switches, or the panel is a fire hazard. Leave the area and call 911, then call us immediately.

Flickering or Dimming Lights

Flickering lights may indicate loose connections, failing components, or overloaded circuits that need professional evaluation.

Partial or Total Power Loss

If part or all of your home loses power, we'll identify whether it's a utility issue, panel problem, or wiring fault.

Hot Outlets or Switches

Outlets or switches that feel warm to the touch indicate dangerous electrical resistance and require immediate professional repair.

What to Do in an Electrical Emergency

Your safety is our top priority. If you experience an electrical emergency, follow these steps:

  • Stay calm and avoid touching: Do not touch sparking outlets, hot switches, or the electrical panel.
  • Turn off power if safe: If you can safely access the breaker panel, switch off the problematic circuit.
  • Call 911 if there's fire: If you see flames, smoke, or a burning smell, evacuate and call emergency services first.
  • Keep everyone away: Do not allow others, especially children and pets, to approach the problem area.
  • Call us immediately: Once you're safe, contact MRK Electric at (425) 766-4793 for emergency service.
  • Don't attempt DIY fixes: Electrical work is dangerous. Leave it to licensed professionals.
