Is It Time?

Signs Your Panel Needs an Upgrade

Most homes built before 1990 were wired for a fraction of today's electrical demands. If any of these sound familiar, a panel upgrade could be the smartest investment you make in your home.

Breakers Trip Repeatedly

If circuit breakers trip regularly under normal household loads, your panel is struggling to keep up with demand.

Only 100-Amp Service

A 100-amp panel cannot safely support an EV charger, heat pump, or hot tub alongside your existing circuits.

Fuses Instead of Breakers

Fuse panels are outdated and often cannot support modern appliances. Many insurers now require replacement before issuing coverage.

Planning a Major Upgrade

Adding an EV charger, hot tub, mini split, or home addition almost always requires additional panel capacity.

Our Process

How a Panel Upgrade Works

A panel upgrade is a multi-step process. Here's exactly what happens when you hire MRK Electric.

1

Load Calculation

We assess your current electrical load and future needs to determine the right panel size — typically 200 amp for most residential upgrades.

2

Utility Coordination

We coordinate with your utility provider (PSE or Seattle City Light) for service upgrade if needed, well before the work day.

3

Permit Application

We pull all required permits from your local jurisdiction — Issaquah, Bellevue, Redmond, or wherever you are.

4

Panel Replacement

Old panel removed, new code-compliant panel installed. All circuits reconnected, properly labeled, and torqued to spec.

5

Final Inspection

County inspector verifies all work. We're present for the inspection and handle any corrections on the spot.

6

Power Restored & Tested

Every circuit is tested, GFCI/AFCI breakers verified, and you get a full walkthrough of your new panel before we leave.

Common Questions

Panel Upgrade FAQ

How long does a panel upgrade take?

▼
Most panel upgrades are completed in one day. The power will be off for several hours while the work is done, then fully restored before we leave.

Do I need to be home during the upgrade?

▼
It's helpful but not always necessary. We'll walk you through the new panel at the end of the job, so we recommend being available then. We'll coordinate around your schedule.

What panel brands are unsafe? Do you replace Zinsco or Federal Pacific panels?

▼
Yes. Zinsco, Federal Pacific (Stab-Lok), Pushmatic, and some older brands have documented safety issues. We replace these with modern Eaton or Square D panels that meet current NEC code.

Will I need a permit and inspection?

▼
Yes — always. Panel upgrades require permits and a final inspection in every Washington jurisdiction. We handle the entire permit process and are present at the inspection.

Can I get a panel upgrade and EV charger installed at the same time?

▼
Absolutely — it's the most efficient approach. We can upgrade your panel and install a dedicated EV charger circuit in the same visit, saving you time and a second permit fee.
